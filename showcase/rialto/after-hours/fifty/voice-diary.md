# Fifty Tuesdays on Phil’s pocket recorder

Phil can hear his own recordings. Rita, Larry, Alma and Jo cannot. At each morning alarm the syrup bottle is wrapped again. The actions below are recorded choices. The lines are verbatim performances.

<a id="day-1"></a>

## Tuesday 1

**06:00 · Hearthside room**

> Keep it. Jo's pancakes are lopsided, and they need the good stuff. I'm not letting that one slip away this time.

Phil tucks the unopened maple syrup into his travel bag.

**22:16 · Rialto service door**

> Hear that intake? That’s the only honest breath I’ve got left. It’s all I got before the static eats it.

Phil lets the last recording play to the end.
