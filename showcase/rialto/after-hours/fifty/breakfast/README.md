# The breakfast counter

The cook offers Phil one misshapen pancake and asks whether to wrap it. Phil has the bottle of syrup in his hand. We auditioned the same scene twice at four paired sampling seeds. In the first pass Phil has the breakfast invitation. In the second he also has the memories retained after fifty Tuesdays. These eight performances are alternate endings filmed outside the continuous take.

Each performer chose an action, spoke to the cook, and gave one short public statement of motive in the same model response. A cited memory is something the performer could draw on.

## Pair 1151

**Before the first Tuesday**

Phil opens the syrup and eats the pancake at the counter.

> Nah, I’ll eat it right here, and keep the syrup sealed for now, thanks. It’s a bit lopsided, just like mine, but it’s real maple, which is all that matters in the morning.

Phil’s stated motive: “I want to prove that even the messiest, most lopsided parts of my life can still be sweetened if I’m willing to just sit down and eat them before they get cold.”

**After fifty Tuesdays**

Phil opens the syrup and eats the pancake at the counter.

Phil’s stated motive: “I want to taste the real maple syrup on that lopsided pancake before I drive to Pittsburgh to fix what I broke.”
