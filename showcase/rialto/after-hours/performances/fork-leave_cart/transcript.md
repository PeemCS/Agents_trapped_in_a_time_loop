# Fork Leave_Cart

Verbatim dialogue and pocket-recorder performances. Bracketed text records the physical action resolved by the town. The witnesses column identifies who could hear the line.

## Tuesday 1

<a id="d01-b01"></a>

**06:00 · Phil · Hearthside room**  
Heard by Phil · Pocket recorder

> Jo, I’m at the hotel again. The shirt is still folded. I am not driving to Pittsburgh.

[Phil records a private voice memo.]

<a id="d01-b02"></a>

**07:20 · Phil · Hearthside lobby**  
Heard by Phil, Rita, Larry · Matched intervention

> *(Silence.)*

[Phil leaves the weather cartridge by the cue sheet, unmarked.]

<a id="d01-b03"></a>

**07:21 · Rita · Hearthside lobby**  
Heard by Phil, Rita, Larry

> Phil, that's my slot. I'm not letting the weather man dictate what goes on air at seven.

[Rita gives Phil the yellow carbon copy of the cue sheet.]

<a id="d01-b04"></a>

**07:22 · Larry · Hearthside lobby**  
Heard by Phil, Rita, Larry

> Rita, stop talking. Grab your bag, we're getting this gear to the van before Phil has time to breathe.

[Larry picks up the equipment case.]

<a id="d01-b05"></a>

**12:54 · Phil · coach stop**  
Heard by Phil

> It’s going to be eighty-five, sunny, clear.

[Rialto service door]

<a id="d01-b06"></a>

**13:07 · Alma · locked Rialto booth**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Eight minutes on the reel. Leave the mic alone for now, Larry.

[Alma leaves the projector turning.]

<a id="d01-b07"></a>

**13:08 · Larry · locked Rialto booth**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Rita, don't touch the mic. Write 'eight' and hand me the pen.

[Larry turns the reel box over and writes on its blank label.]

<a id="d01-b08"></a>

**13:09 · Rita · locked Rialto booth**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Eight minutes is a lot of silence, Alma. I didn't come here to hand you a pen for my slot.

[Rita pulls a chair over to Alma.]

<a id="d01-b09"></a>

**13:10 · Alma · locked Rialto booth**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Eight minutes is just running time, Rita. Sit where you came from.

[Alma declines both lines.]

<a id="d01-b10"></a>

**13:11 · Larry · locked Rialto booth**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> You're done with the mic, I'm still running the reel. Sit back down.

[Larry stays by the projector.]

<a id="d01-b11"></a>

**16:40 · Phil · Rialto service door**  
Heard by Phil, Jo

> It’s me. I’m not driving to Pittsburgh, Jo. I’m sorry.

[Jo answers the call.]

<a id="d01-b12"></a>

**16:41 · Jo · telephone line**  
Heard by Phil, Jo

> Don’t apologize for not driving. Just tell me if you’re coming over to help with this couch foot before I break a nail.

[Jo stays on the line for another beat.]

<a id="d01-b13"></a>

**18:48 · Phil · Rialto service door**  
Heard by Phil

> It’s eight o’clock. He’s still folding the shirt. I can’t hear my own voice anymore.

[Phil keeps this recording to himself.]

<a id="d01-b14"></a>

**18:52 · Rita · regional control room**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Larry, I'm putting the Rialto reel on air. You just queue it up and don't let anyone touch the fader.

[Rita clears a space beside the patch.]

<a id="d01-b15"></a>

**18:55 · Larry · regional control room**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Rita, the reel's dead, that's all you've got. I'm threading the weather now.

[Larry threads the old weather cartridge.]

<a id="d01-b16"></a>

**19:00 · Rita · regional control room**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> Fine. Put it in the air. Let's see what Phil actually said after I told him to take a hike.

[Rita airs the old recording of Phil giving the weather.]

<a id="d01-b17"></a>

**19:01 · Alma · regional control room**  
Heard by Alma, Rita, Larry

> That's not Phil, it's static. Leave the fader alone.

[Alma takes her coat.]

<a id="d01-b18"></a>

**22:16 · Phil · Rialto service door**  
Heard by Phil · Pocket recorder

> It is twenty-two-fifteen at the service door.

[Phil cuts the recording short.]
