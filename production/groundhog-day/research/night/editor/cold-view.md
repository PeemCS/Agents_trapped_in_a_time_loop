I screened A first, then B, from chronological images at two-second intervals. I read the captions, clock, character tags and location signs. I revisited A's short windowsill transitions at half-second intervals. Both files contain audio tracks. My screening interface omitted the audio when I attempted to play it, so I cannot judge voice, sound cues, mix or real-time delivery. Times below are approximate elapsed video times. This records one agent's editorial reading before seeing any text packet.

## Screen A · 1m34s

- **Where the day ended and restarted.** Phil took the one o'clock bus out of Mill Creek around 0:22–0:28. He made it. Around 0:29–0:41 he was alone at the wall phone in Pittsburgh Arrivals at seven in the evening. The sign, dark city skyline and change of location tag all registered. Around 0:42–0:46 the alarm caption appeared over Phil in the familiar Mill Creek inn, February 2, 6 AM. Rita's opening greeting returned. I understood that even leaving town could not get him to Wednesday. The final shot at 1:28–1:34 leaves him in Pittsburgh again.
- **His choice and his next move.** He wanted the bus and used his paid ticket even with the crew still working. He called his sister to confirm he would help move her couch. After waking he rode out again, then proposed a test himself, a pencil cross on the hotel windowsill around 0:53–1:04. He marked it again after a later reset. By 1:12–1:23 he changed his morning, walked to Bea's diner and cleared a rack, then boarded the bus again. I took the plate as a small voluntary act. I could see little of whom it helped or why that morning moved him to try it.
- **What I would send.** Around 0:28–0:47, from the phone in Pittsburgh through Rita's repeated greeting. The couch is the first thing that made me want Wednesday to happen for him. A promise to someone outside the loop makes the return to the inn sting. I would include the location sign and the first morning after the call in the same clip.
- **Who else decided anything.** Larry owns the choice to roll on the festival (0:04–0:11). He is doing his job in the square while Phil pursues another goal. Rita's greeting helps sell the reset. A brief caption says she inspects the sill, but I could say little about her own stakes. The named figures staying behind on the overhead map helped identify who actually went to Pittsburgh. Phil addressing Rita while he appears alone at the diner briefly confused me.
- **What I lost.** The line “Try something” reads like an unassigned instruction. The first “unmarked” inspection comes before the first clear pencil-cross beat. On the following morning the caption skips straight to making another cross. I could follow the idea of the experiment more confidently than its result. “1 rack(s) remain” made me think about a counter just when I wanted to see someone benefit. The final river line gave me a sense of place but little new feeling after several arrivals.

## Screen B · 1m14s

- **Where the day ended and restarted.** Phil ended the fifth Tuesday at the Mill Creek depot around 0:52–0:57. “Pittsburgh 1:00 PM” is on the *departure* sign. The town is still outside the window, the Mill Creek tag stays put, and the clock says 7 PM. At about 0:58–1:01 he woke back at the Mill Creek inn at 6 AM on Tuesday. Around 1:02–1:14 he headed to the depot again and ended looking at the timetable again. I saw no successful trip to Pittsburgh.
- **His choice and his next move.** Around 0:44–0:51 Phil finally left the lobby for the depot, despite Rita wanting to sort out the band. He read the schedule that evening. The next day he made essentially the same trip. I couldn't tell whether he knowingly missed boarding, had arrived too late, or mistook reading the timetable for taking the bus. His opening goal was clear. The logistics of the failure were murky.
- **What I would send.** Around 0:27–0:34, Larry with his camera lowered after using the battery on the festival. Calling himself “just a listener for the rest of the night” gave him a voice and a consequence I could picture. The earlier shot of him spending his last charged take at 0:16–0:19 earns the line, although the day counter jumps between the two shots.
- **Who else decided anything.** Larry elects to film, Rita prioritizes the town band's slot, Phil wants his coach. Each has a separate task. Rita has a more specific concern here than in A, but the band never materializes. The overhead map shows Rita and Larry staying in town as Phil heads for the coach. The parked vehicle beside the PHIL tag briefly made me think he had boarded. The station's place label and departure arrow corrected that reading.
- **What I lost.** Phil asks whether they're in the lobby while he visibly stands there (0:20–0:27). At 7 PM he sounds pleased to discover the 1 PM coach (“Afternoon coach, right there. Good.”). I couldn't tell if this was a deliberate joke about having tomorrow to try again. Repeating the same timetable at the end left me with no new decision to anticipate. I felt more for Larry's lost evening than for Phil's missed departure.

**Cold preference.** A. The call, promise and reset held together even when the pencil business became hard to follow. In B the strongest miniature story belonged to the cameraman.
