# Winter-town blind screening protocol · version 1

Freeze before the new actor runs. The unit of assessment is **one independently started world**, with the director choosing at most one excerpt per world. Report all roots. Group alternate memory treatments of the same seed as paired takes. Log technical failures and caps with their own denominators. An attractive shorter excerpt is eligible for editing only if it belongs to a mechanically valid world.

## Before unblinding

A packer assigns opaque world labels, strips model, seed, API metadata and internal reflection, and preserves every event in chronological order with a stable event number. Retain the actual character names, locations, quotes, clock, actions and receipts. Earlier Ellis rehearsals and newer Phil roots can therefore be distinguished by casting, a known source of imperfect blinding. Review paired Phil conditions before viewing the lookup key. The primary evaluator sees no model labels or source file names. On a machine-only pass, explicitly identify the evaluator as an agent.

A reader records these seven judgments. Cite an event number or a consecutive pair for every positive mark, and quote the relevant utterance exactly when a judgment depends on words.

| Measure | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Legible objective | No grounded objective | Stated or reasonably inferable goal | Action pursues it at meaningful cost or forecloses an alternative |
| Changed behavior | Repetition, generic variation or menu drift | Deliberate new action connected to a prior observed event | Follow-through or falsifiable test followed by an informed revision |
| Film recognition | Holiday only, ordinary time-loop deduction, or none | Names or unmistakably identifies the movie in relation to the actor's experience | Explicit comparison changes a subsequent chosen action |
| Social agency | Two other people absent or ornamental | A distinct other character independently chooses and affects a consequence | An independent choice is respected, changes a later opportunity or incurs a visible cost |
| Line-to-action grounding | Words misstate the physical outcome or known facts | No material contradiction, but words add little | A short utterance contributes a grounded observation, prediction or reversal |
| Emotional legibility | Uninterpretable, or sadness inferred only from repetition/silence | Observable change in response to a concrete disappointment/opportunity | Changed behavior plus a grounded line or costly choice makes the interpretation intelligible |
| Editorial yield | No supportable story sequence | One anchored image/line | One clear setup → voluntary act → consequence → later response in the same world |

An independent claim may also be marked **unobservable** if the recording contains no relevant opportunity or the view is incomplete. Report the opportunity denominator. Do not convert these into scores of zero for means. Technical and emotional categories remain separate. A small exploratory sample supports a comparison of these particular takes.

For spontaneous recognition, separately inspect all actor-visible prompts through the first claimed recognition and earlier utterances by every agent. Record who first supplied the analogy. Grade a date, a reference to the animal or even the phrase “Groundhog Day” in isolation as a holiday reference. A model naming the title in an isolated quiz is a capability control. If the prompt archive is incomplete, mark prompting provenance unverified.

## Physical eligibility

A coach payoff needs a committed travel action with arrival in Pittsburgh, a subsequent observed event or undisputed end-of-evening record there, and a later morning whose pre-action origin is the inn. Ending a take in Pittsburgh leaves the return unobserved. The first act that morning may leave the inn immediately. Reconstruct the morning from its pre-action snapshot. Capture the return as a distinct shot. Never require the model to say it is upset.

A claim about new behavior needs a *named prior observation*. If the actor says it boarded while the committed act stayed in town, play the actual act and label the words as speech. A visual metaphor must never be the only evidence for a physical fact. Avoid explaining a missing motive with voiceover. An extra-world fork is separately captioned.

## Human screening, when people are available

Screen once without narration and once muted. Ask “Where did the reporter end the day?”, “Where did he wake up?”, “Which choice was actually his?”, “What did he do afterward?” and “Which single moment would you send to someone?” Record verbatim answers before the reveal. Keep agent editorial judgments and human audience feedback separate. Variable seed and actor identity are part of the conditions for any later comparison.
