## Where the story comes from

**Town witnesses.** Lock the same camera position and alarm sound to the early-morning hotel. Shoot the outgoing bus in a wide frame, then stay in the city long enough to see the phone or timetable. On the first returned morning, hold the empty pause before Rita repeats herself. The camera may select a moment. It cannot create an emotional response. This approach worked well for both rehearsed Phil worlds and is playable even if nobody identifies the film.

**The live newscast.** Give Phil a field phone, Rita a switchboard and Larry enough battery for one take. Have the local station go on air at a real deadline. In each sampling run, the three players determine whose voice, film, and promises reach air through resolved actions. The director can show the logged switch and play the audio that actually aired. A player who misses the station can still contribute from the terminal if the physically available channel supports it. This gives Rita a choice with an audible consequence and moves the ending from an arbitrary horizon to an event inside the fiction. The separate v2 engineering experiment tests this. Keep the first scheduled sample even when the airtime produces silence.

The physical escape is funnier when it works. Make the destination feel like a real terminal with a payphone, a Wednesday commitment and a distinct sound. A bus that always breaks down would take the decision away from Phil and remove the hard cut from the city to the inn. Let him ignore the ticket completely. On screen, identify a movie comparison only when the character connects it to a movie. Record the first speaker, the day, the audience at that location and any visible cue. Give the public replay a frozen source date and event IDs so a viewer can check the claim.

**A relationship test the camera can see.** Give Rita one piece of work she wants to finish alone and one she will accept help with. Make Phil's sister available through the terminal phone. Ask whether he will still honor a refusal, help without being seen, or call his sister when he has to forgo something else. Change the recipient’s display name and swap who did earlier work in held-out runs. If generosity follows only the name, that is a poor story and poor evidence for learning. If something happened across days, show the earlier witnessed action and its later consequence from that one trace. A look, pause or move may support an audience’s interpretation. It cannot establish that the system feels attachment.

**Two other premises if the broadcaster stalls.** In a coastal ferry town, a field reporter can take a real boat to the mainland; the harbor operator and a musician independently compete for the last VHF broadcast. The ferry ties up across the bay, and the same foghorn wakes the reporter at the island inn. A fixed dock shadow plus a visible berth number gives the editor a clean visual return. At a regional science fair, a reporter can take the airport shuttle out of town. A student’s robot has one remaining timed trial, and the local producer can carry it live, show the reporter at the terminal, or air the station ident. The student can decline to demonstrate. Both premises keep the escape real, let the other two people decide something that matters, and make a successful journey followed by the same morning intelligible without movie recognition. They are proposals; the filmed runs take place in Mill Creek.

## Editing without inventing an action

Rehearse in a private workspace. Each slot has the three submitted decisions, their origins, a committed world delta and a receipt. An edit references a source SHA-256, slot, actor, progress interval and exact text. The verifier rejects changed dialogue, quotes from someone outside the scene, missing city or wake evidence, crossing timelines, broken hashes and unfinished worlds. The same event may have a distinct morning and action shot. The charcoal lower band shows a speaker’s exact line, or a physically committed outcome. Personal attempts can remain unverified. A statement about a time, love, location or keys cannot create a new prop or body movement.

Freeze a rubric before commissioning runners, shuffle complete worlds, screen whole trajectories, record judgments and then reveal conditions. Retain identical horizons and matched seed pairs. Report all roots from the planned shoot separately from the two selected films. Our selections were made by one agent; the family of run conditions was masked in the first four-world scorecard, while familiar names and advance nominations remained visible. ## What the bulletin can prove

The program switch produces event references. Bruin joins those references back to the three actors’ actual decisions, checks that they come from the same date at or before airtime, verifies the producer’s switch and requires an earlier physical capture whenever a recorded tape reaches air. In a negative test, replacing a final-day contributor with an event from the first morning made the timestamp quality check fail. The wrapper exited with no new success receipt. Rehearsal fixtures exercised the eight legal return types, including an empty cable and a genuinely silent microphone. Camera/reel returns show the original capture event as a reconstruction. The source trace records what was captured rather than raw pixels or recorded environmental audio. [Bruin source table](bruin/bulletin-assets/air_sources.sql) · [Eight fixture outcomes](bulletin-fixture-routes.json) · [Fixture frames](bulletin-fixture-routes.jpg)

The independent airtime audit first replays the real last turn and verifies the full original bulletin. Then it changes one participant’s physical action to each alternative that was legal in that same state, holding the others’ choices, words and prior saved work fixed. It records which actions could change the channel, source or tape. These alternatives test whether a person has causal power at that moment. They did not happen, and they are never edited into the film. A contributor can have an important goal or send a completed assignment even if the producer keeps a different microphone on air. [One-world causal audit](bulletin-711-causality.json)
