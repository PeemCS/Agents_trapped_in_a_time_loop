## Why this take

We considered the three completed 721 conditions, each extending the same real departure, call and hotel return. In the ordinary arm, Phil’s first unspoken model output described the situation as Groundhog Day. On the first evening he explicitly identified his role as the movie character. In the library arm his first-afternoon note identified the character from the film after a diegetic 1993-comedy clue. Both of those Phils chose the coach on all three afternoons. In both, one evening phone call was followed by two terminal-bench sleeps. The private-hypothesis arm alone chose a film-linked piano rehearsal. Its other two afternoons ended in Pittsburgh. No Phil/Rita dance occurred in the nine evenings. Full movie-character names and remembered repetition are cultural cues even in the ordinary arm. All three roots are one shared seed and the selection was informed by seeing the first two arms.
