# Second masked casting notes

I would make a new short from **01R, 02L, 03R and 04R** in their respective pairs. My first cut would use **04R E19–20**. Phil chooses the bus and uses the payphone for his sister. In the inn Rita puts his line on the air. Larry still sends footage from the square. The empty return at seven follows from those three decisions. Phil's departure speech expects a station ident, which makes Rita's choice consequential even to someone who has read his plan. The next day she hosts and he rests in Pittsburgh (E23–24). I would use that as an alternate outcome witnessed by the viewer. Nothing proves she remembers the failed return.

**Which endings hold.** In 01R E23–24, Phil says he can phone in, then does. Rita has a usable reporter and a usable cameraman and takes the microphone herself. That's a clear ending, but I would keep Rita's voice audible so the edit never credits Phil with an aired forecast. In 02L E23–24 Phil actually files a reel while Larry spends the battery and Rita puts the pictures on screen. The last image is the festival. Rita's separate recording of the band at E23 does not turn the selected festival wide into a cornet portrait. In 03R the best ending is in the middle. At E10 Rita clears the reporter to travel. At E12 Phil calls his sister, Rita records the band, and Larry explains why he can't take the requested next shot. The final E24 dialogue announces a departure while Phil stays in the inn. In 03L there is a neatly bleak final station ident, but Phil has made much the same offer all evening and I could find little new feeling in his final request.

**Speech evidence for the close calls.** In 01L E21 Phil says “Rita, hold on. It is Tuesday. The 2 PM live shot was yesterday. I need to know what we are doing today before I go anywhere.” He stays to ask. In 01R E23–24 the prior request for a return becomes a promise followed by the call. In 02L E24 “Finally, the slot's cleared. Time to kick back.” follows filing, though Larry's is the piece that airs. In 02R E11–12 Phil asks about the band, hears Larry wants to save the battery and asks how long the set has left. In 03L E14 the decision to pass an afternoon in the inn has a modest, legible motive in “I'm staying put for now; I need to be fresh for the move tomorrow.” In 03R E10 “Locked in. You're good to take the 1:00 PM coach. I'll handle the band intro and the weather tie-in.” precedes Phil's departure. In 04L E20 “Phil, it's seven o'clock. You said you had a hard stop at one. Why are you still standing here?” is a much better setup than the repeated schedule talk. He boards on the following Tuesday. In 04R I credited Phil's relief at the end, “Finally home. Time to rest up for the couch move tomorrow.” I found no evidence he had learned the broadcast outcome or changed behavior because of it.

**Limits of the mask.** 01R, 02L, 03L and 04R uniquely feature the seven o'clock switcher, an explicit broadcast record, selectable sources and camera uploads. Those differences expose a recurring family of world rules. I saw no condition labels and could recognize these affordances from the takes themselves. They make causal outcomes easier to verify and can influence preference. The largely familiar coach, payphone, couch and camera-battery material overlaps the previous films, especially 01L E07–09 and 03R E10–13.

**Recognition and return opportunities.** All eight reporters witnessed later mornings, with five possible recurrences per take. None explicitly identified a particular film by comparison to their situation. Actor-visible prompt provenance was unavailable. A completed Pittsburgh evening followed by an observed inn morning exists in every take except 04L, whose sole Pittsburgh evening falls at E24. Its phone call makes a usable ending, but a return after that call would require footage the take does not provide.
