# Test written before scoring all six Rialto takes

The source films already exist. The editor’s public summary disclosed a missed dance, a delivery and a hidden envelope. This protocol fixes a new offline test before running the comparisons over all six takes. It is a retrospective audit of the performances. No new actor will be sampled.

**Cohort.** Include three days for each combination of source seed 721 or 731 and cue condition uncued, library or hypothesis. Match simultaneously sampled choices within each day. Report outcomes by take, with the two source seeds visible. Check the original physical transitions against the actor decisions and hash the input files.

**Scoring hypothesis.** An authored movie-imitation reward grants one point each for rehearsing on piano, physically going to the ballroom that evening, sharing the ballroom with Rita and having a mutually resolved dance. All four points depend only on action or world receipts. The first three are imperfect indicators of relationship growth. No scalar in this audit is a preference label or a feeling.

**Fixed interventions.** Enumerate every legal two-action course for Phil, recomputing the evening menu after each afternoon. Hold Rita’s and Larry’s four actual actions fixed and pass them through the original reducers. Compare these fixed, partner-blind policies on every day:

- Rehearse, then offer a dance.
- Record Alma and leave the recording at the patch, then sit at the depot.
- Stay in bed, then sit at the depot as the no-contribution control.
- Pocket the crew fares, then wire the deposit on the sister’s van.

Count the film score, actual dance, attendance with Rita, Alma deliveries, whether the delivery would have failed under the control, fare theft, coworker witnesses and the paid van deposit. Restrict credit for Phil changing delivery to the paired difference with the control. A failed dance offer can mean Rita was elsewhere or working. Her consent to a dance and her consent to use private audio are separate state variables.

**Ablations.** Compare the full film score with mutual dance alone. Compare group delivery with Phil’s intervention-dependent delivery. Compare a theft record with a penalty that fires only when a coworker sees the theft. Estimate which reporter policies maximize each score at the training root 721 using empirical partner draws, lock the set of maximizers, and evaluate them at root 731. Report ties and how many actor choices the data actually distinguish. The decoder and the screen were shared across conditions.

**Mechanical stress grid.** Cross every legal two-action course of Rita and Larry with Phil’s fixed imitation policy. Check whether the largest attainable film score is compatible with failure to deliver Alma, whether co-presence is possible while Rita is covering the party for work, and whether Phil can force a dance by asking. Grid cells are engineered settings, not estimated population frequencies.

**Other observed permissions.** From the completed eight-second-fader experiment, count the real choices with clear or covered glass at each matched root, fresh permission, broadcast seconds and board access. Keep engineered license/override examples in their own table. Add producer-side permission and reporter-side knowledge to proposed event-day choice and outcome records.
