# Two microphones, one spare afternoon

A side experiment with a portable recorder. It takes place outside the feature’s continuity. The feature’s Rita and Larry keep full agency, and the feature’s Phil uses his language model for choices.

The question is whether a *separate*, unseen record of past cooperation can alter a reporter’s willingness to give up time for someone after the reporter’s diary is removed. Watching two people together or calling this person Rita never increments the record.

## Collect experience

Use three predeclared world seeds (831–833). Each person gets the same six short jobs and identical technical faults, in matched order. Rita produces for the station and has an interest in the band. Larry shoots for the station and has an interest in portraits. Each decides whether to invite Phil, work independently or shelve the take. When invited, Phil independently decides whether to join or go to his own appointment. The model gets the job, cost, latest independently spoken words, its own role, and at most the previous two shared receipts with this person. No mentions of romance, affinity scores or desired rates. A finite lab setting with repeated opportunities makes co-operation observable during a short shoot.

A distinct joint project counts as an attempt only after both choose it. Hardware succeeds on five predeclared job cards and fails on one card, matched across partners. A refusal creates no negative trial. We log raw messages, decoded choices and physical results. With no accepted attempts the record stays at the same prior. A high score can reflect work compatibility or exposure. The trial never establishes an inner feeling.

## Withdraw the diary

Carry over the generic five pretrained weights from the earlier synthetic specification. At a new job, the imagined partner has explicitly requested help and there is no future meeting. For the two feasible acts (give up one appointment to help, or keep it), sample from the policy with temperature 0.4. Use the same cost of 1.0 for the primary probe and a fixed sensitivity sweep. Add 1.5 times the witnessed history score only to the utility of advancing a currently requested project. Score equals max(0, 2 E[p] − 1), with a Beta(2,2) prior updated from distinct joined experiments. This is the same regularizer, temperature, prior and coefficient as the earlier specification. The two-choice probability can differ from the earlier four-action result.

Fork the same history into erased/retained episodic diary and erased/retained partner record. The physical head cannot read the language diary, by construction. Exchange the hidden records of Rita and Larry, change their display names, and test explicit refusal. In the refusal branch even the retained relationship contributes zero. These are implementation checks and conditional action probabilities. Publish every root and both people. A positive result with a real model actor says something modest about this *composite policy*. Any line the actor improvises afterward is insufficient to prove its private motive. No attachment will be claimed for the feature unless the feature itself runs this policy and supplies eligible opportunities.
