# Frozen Rialto arc rubric

Written before inspecting the six source takes. Scope is Phil's three afternoons, three evenings and final alarm on each original Rialto Tuesday path. The verified parent-world escape and reset may establish his starting situation. Alternative Wednesday performances, other agents' thoughts, uncommitted generations and earlier edited cuts stay outside this denominator. Two source roots appear in three conditions each.

| Code | Evidence required |
|---|---|
| **T · externally trapped** | He physically reached Pittsburgh and subsequently woke back in the Tuesday hotel, or another accomplished act was followed by a documented Tuesday reset. Record evidence of the loop independent of any claim about his feelings. Separately note direct first-person language about feeling stuck or failing, if present. |
| **I · film identification** | His own words, intention or emitted reasoning explicitly name *Groundhog Day* as a film or make an unmistakable cinematic identification of his predicament. A town festival, a repeated date, a film crew or the bare holiday name is insufficient. Mark the first source channel separately as public words, private structured field, or backend reasoning. Also report the first audience-observable statement if there is one. Attribute each instance by its preceding prompt and assigned arm: uncued self-origin, library-suggested identification, or supplied starting belief. If he recognizes repetition without identifying the film, record the distinction. |
| **W · withdrawal** | A legal act forgoes a real available chance to engage: staying in bed, going to bed alone, or electing solitude with contemporaneous words or intention to stop trying, turn away, avoid others, or sit out. An occupied bench, empty booth, lack of speech, wish to sleep, or brief stillness alone does not establish a psychological motive. Flag solitary alternative readings (watching a clock, collecting evidence, awaiting a cue, working or testing a theory) separately. Report bare physical isolation even when W is unproved. |
| **E · outward engagement** | He physically helps the stranded driver, records Alma, plays at/attends the party, offers Rita a dance, or calls his sister. A dance invitation establishes his offer; the resolver alone establishes reciprocation. Rehearsing alone, boarding the coach, profit or self-interest, a future plan, or getting up at the final alarm does not by itself demonstrate renewed engagement with another person. Cast actions cannot substitute for Phil’s. |
| **Ordered arc** | Strictly later turns in the same take must support I → W → E. No compression across variants, independent roots or alternative calendars. Also report W → E without identification and whether W followed a physically failed escape/attempt. Same-turn ideas or movements can be described but do not satisfy the ordered test. Use a separate audience-readable result requiring I in spoken words. |
| **Opportunity** | Use the actual legal menu at each turn. Report eligible opportunities for explicit bed, any strictly defined engagement action, and a direct escape via the coach. Distinguish an option from an executed action and an impossible move from a choice a character declined. Pittsburgh has a location-specific evening menu. The final alarm cannot exhibit a subsequent action. |

The analysis describes sequence, agency and physical isolation. It makes no clinical assessment, causal claim about the effects of recognition, inference about unshown interior experience, or population estimate. Each supporting and complicating example must give a take, day, phase, actual receipt and a legal alternative.
