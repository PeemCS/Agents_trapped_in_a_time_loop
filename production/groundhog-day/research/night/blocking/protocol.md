# Let the actor block the scene

A reporter who can see a menu often describes his relationship to the menu in his thinking. Can he perform a more interesting hotel wake if he chooses his first visible gesture before somebody has to map it onto the simulator? This experiment keeps the coach trip, the exact witnessed morning, the actor identity and the decoded seed. It changes the acting interface.

Use the four physically settled, first eligible post-Pittsburgh wakes from the sealed thinking-mode audition. Use its two seeds and both thinking flags, its native 27B digest, 0.8 temperature, 8,192 context and 1,536-token prediction cap. The menu control comes from that previously declared audition. The alternate version keeps all the same personal context except the menu and the menu instructions. Ask for one immediate visible action, optional words, and a short optional practical intention. Do not add a direction about time, romance, movies or psychology. Only the performer's interface changes here. Rita and Larry's simultaneous original choices remain fixed at this checkpoint.

A separate call maps an action onto the ordinary physical action space. It sees the present physical setup, proposed gesture and possible actions. It has no earlier memories, reasoning trace, actor intention, desired ending, or permission to invent a destination or perform another person's decision. An unmappable act stays visible to an editor as an attempt. It receives no simulated effect. Keep the raw proposals, emitted thinking, receipts and any mapper abstentions. A mapped act still has to pass the old engine. These are alternate takes of a single morning per root. They are not four new feature films.

Inspect the paired frequency of complete answers, concrete visible choices beyond walking or talking, reference to actual remembered events, denial or generic movie/time vocabulary, representability and refused effects. Use each world as the independent unit. A visually interesting gesture can be unsupported in the current renderer. Keep that distinction in the report.

After observing all 16 unconstrained gestures resolve to the same generic physical activity, freeze a follow-up with eight thinking-on draws, on the same four exact wakes and seeds. Retain the ordinary grounded action choice and offer a second optional field for a visible gesture during that act. Change only the original restriction on what can go in that field. Repeat the 1,536-token cap. Resolve the physical act through the original engine, preserve the gestural proposal for the animator, and keep invalid or incomplete draws.
