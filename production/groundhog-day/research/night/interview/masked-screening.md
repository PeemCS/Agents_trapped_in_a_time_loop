One agent read the four matched triples in packet order. I judged the beats on the page. Timing, eye contact and vocal inflection remain prospective acting choices.

| 04 | A | She seals the copy and then leaves it alone. Phil's actual delivery remains outside the recorded beats. | The change from “Go.” to “Phil.” makes a playable turn. Blinding was partial in practice. Repeated Rivoli references, very clipped replies and unusually long narration make some performances sound related across triples. I had no model or condition key. The master stayed with Rita in every take, so none of the votes depended on her surrendering the original. A blank words field records no line and leaves the performance of silence unresolved.
