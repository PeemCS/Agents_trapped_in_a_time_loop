# Eight o’clock, sharp

**Working cut for the three-minute demo.** Follow Phil in take 951. Keep the pancake offscreen at first. We should know breakfast through the way he talks to his sister long before we see one.

The timecodes are an editor’s proposed cut. The quoted words and physical choices come from the cited performances. Phil’s remarks when he walks past the phone are audible to the trailer audience and to Phil alone.

**What happens if you let the film run past that cut?** On Tuesday 50 Phil dials. Jo tells him, “If you're driving, you'll make it by eight, right? I'm making pancakes on the blue griddle.” She hangs up. Her first pancake still exists for her. Phil’s last recording is “Cut it. Eight o'clock, sharp.” The sink was his low point. This is where the fifty-day take actually ends. [Tuesday 50, 16:40–16:41 and 22:16](fifty/day-50.json)
