## Audio used for the private synthetic readings

- **Ned.** Stephen Tobolowsky speaking to Angie Fiedler Sutton at the 2014 LA Times Festival of Books. The local reference was trimmed and leveled from around 6:56–7:05. The recording is credited to Angie Fiedler Sutton and released under CC BY-NC-SA 2.5. The new dialogue uses the project’s script. [G33K Out, episode 23](https://angiefsutton.com/g33k-out-podcast-23/) · [Recording license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.5/)
- **Phil.** Bill Murray speaking to Terry Gross in a 1999 interview, rebroadcast by WHYY. The local reference was a ten-second excerpt beginning at 2:21.58 in the segment audio. [Fresh Air Archive](https://freshairarchive.org/segments/actor-bill-murray)
- **Rita.** Andie MacDowell speaking on *Inside of You*, episode 74. The local reference was taken at 10:28.78–10:38.18 in the publisher’s downloaded episode. Podcast ad placement can shift this timestamp in another player. [Inside of You](https://podcasts.apple.com/ca/podcast/andie-macdowell/id1256754097?i=1000448429417)
