# Groundhog Day — recording script

## 0:00–0:14 · Tuesday, again

*Snowy main street, through Phil’s eyes. Rita cues the crew. He taps his watch.*

**RITA, aloud** — Thirty more seconds?

**NED, aloud** — Phil? I’ve got a policy that—

*Phil is already past him. Night. Black. The clock flips to six on his right. The same song comes through the radio. His eyes open on three frosted windows.*

**DJ, on the radio** — Rise and shine, campers! Tuesday, February second!

**PHIL, inner voice · recorded thought** — That’s... the Groundhog Day situation. I’m stuck in a loop.

## 0:14–0:28 · The piano

*Phil sits at the upright. His hands find the keys.*

**PHIL, inner voice · recorded intention** — Test the theory. Not the coach. Not the couch. The piano.

*He misses a note and starts over. Continue from later in the same intention.*

**PHIL, inner voice** — I sit down and I play, badly, and I stay until my fingers remember a shape I do not own.

*Cut from his hands on the keys to his hands on his knees. He’s on a depot bench. We hear the dance he’s missing. Clack.*

## 0:28–0:42 · Pittsburgh

*Coach door. Snow passing the window.*

**PHIL, inner voice · recorded intention from the coach take** — The couch is a couch. It does not move itself.

*Pittsburgh, seven that evening. A payphone. We hear only Phil’s side.*

**PHIL, aloud to his sister** — It’s Phil. I’m in Pittsburgh. The van is confirmed for eight A.M. I’ll be there.

*Hold the open line. Clack. The same song. The same hotel ceiling.*

**DJ, on the radio** — Tuesday, February second.

**PHIL, inner voice · scripted** — I was there. She heard me.

## 0:42–0:52 · The crew fares

**PHIL, aloud** — Rita, Larry—see you at the party. I’m heading to Pittsburgh.

*Rita holds the door for Larry. Phil already has a punched ticket. With his other hand, he slips the cash from the crew’s return-fare envelope into his coat. That night the bills are still there.*

## 0:52–1:14 · The curtains

**RITA, aloud** — Morning, Phil.

**PHIL, aloud** — About last—

*She waits. He gives her the weather.*

**PHIL, aloud** — Snow till noon.

*Phil closes the curtains. For the first time the camera leaves his eyes. We see him curled on the bed, still wearing his coat.*

**PHIL, inner voice · recorded thought** — The curtains are closed. I’ve spent the afternoon in bed. Again. The loop has done this to me so many times I can barely feel the texture of the sheets anymore.

*At dusk he ties one shoe. Across town, Rita and Larry are still at work.*

## 1:14–1:34 · The pen

*Phil checks the repaired wheel. A car pulls away. Ned opens his policy book. This time Phil stays.*

**NED, aloud** — It covers the whole year.

**PHIL, aloud** — I’ll take it.

*Ned reaches for his pen. Empty pocket. He walks back to the car. Hold on Phil in the snow. When Ned returns, Phil signs. Phil leaves with his copy. We follow Ned into the hotel.*

**RITA, aloud** — You sold one?

**NED, aloud** — To Phil. I forgot my pen.

*The blue carbon sticks. Ned peels it free, then looks up.*

**NED, aloud** — He waited.

## 1:34–1:48 · Wednesday

*At the station, Rita puts her finger on the switch.*

**RITA, aloud** — I’m putting it on the wire.

*Later, Phil is at the piano with his coat on. Rita pulls up a chair. He plays the phrase again and leaves it unfinished.*

**PHIL, aloud** — There are two more bars.

**RITA, aloud** — Save those for the morning.

*Clack. The same pillow, the same windows. This time the light is warm. Before Phil can tell her the date, Rita speaks from beside him.*

**RITA, aloud** — You owe me two bars.

**DJ, on the radio** — Wednesday.

## 1:48–3:00 · Live demo

**1:48 · Presenter** — Here’s one evening we actually ran. Rita went to dance. Phil had practiced all afternoon.

**1:54 · Presenter** — He meant to test the piano. When he had to choose where to spend the evening, he sat at the depot.

**2:02 · Presenter** — Only Phil and Ned witnessed the sale. Ned telling Rita he waited, and what she remembers on Wednesday, are our next scenes.

**2:10 · Presenter** — The town records who was there. On another take, Ned could tell Rita what he saw. The Band adapter can check that a message reached its intended recipient.

**2:22 · Presenter** — Bruin checks the recorded events in order. A trip can go in the edit when we can trace the arrival, the time spent in Pittsburgh, and the next wake at the inn.

**2:35 · Presenter** — As the writer, I can try an evening the actors passed up. Here they dance. Both of these calendar rules still say Tuesday.

**2:44 · Presenter** — Give Rita the regional line instead. Alma’s daughter hears the interview. Under this delivery rule, the morning changes.

**2:52 · Presenter** — We wrote a man who can remember and a town that keeps going without him. Now we can give Ned someone to tell, and find out what Rita does with it.
