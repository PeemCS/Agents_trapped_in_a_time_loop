# Groundhog Day — standalone trailer shooting script

**Target cut: 3:15 · 24 fps · 21:9 · Punxsutawney, winter 1993.** Times are elapsed minutes and seconds. Shot IDs refer to the supplied board. The coach is Phil’s first test. The added first wake uses the same bedroom setup as the second. The documentary demo is a separate video.

**Public picture edit.** The trailer is silent. Its black cards and Phil’s on-screen thoughts carry the story. The dialogue, radio, and effects cues below describe the performances and their timing. ## S00 · One normal day, then the same morning · 0:00–1:04

**0:00–0:04 · Black.** Quiet bedroom. White type, held long enough to read once.

> We made an agent live through Groundhog Day.

**0:04–0:08 · Second black card.**

> Drawn from the agent’s recorded thoughts and decisions.

Let the words disappear. A plastic split flap lands in the dark, just before the cut.

**0:08–0:13.5 · First wake.** Fixed 14 mm from Phil’s eyes, head sunk into the south pillow. Aim straight ahead at the ceiling and the three frosted bay arches. A seam in the quilt crosses the bottom of frame. His hand flexes once. The radio plays offscreen right. Cut on an ordinary breath.

**RADIO DJ** — “Rise and shine, campers! Tuesday, February second!”

**0:13.5–0:18 · S00-A, weather shoot.** Standing 14 mm POV, looking northeast along the snowy sidewalk. Rita is beside the broadcast camera. She cues the crew, then catches Phil’s eye. His right glove taps the silver watch on his left wrist while she’s still looking at him. Wind brushes the news mic.

**RITA · ALOUD** — “Thirty more seconds?”

**0:18–0:21.5 · S00-B, brick pier.** Continue east at walking speed. Ned steps into Phil’s path and opens his burgundy policy book. Phil slips past his shoulder. Let Ned’s voice drop behind us while his sentence is still going.

**NED · ALOUD** — “Phil? I’ve got a policy that—”

**0:21.5–0:26 · S00-C, bedtime.** Match the corner of Ned’s book to the corner of the patchwork quilt. Same pillow-height 14 mm, now at night. Phil’s hands settle. Hold until the fabric stops moving. Eyes close. Black.

**0:26–0:33 · S00-D/E, second wake.** Replay the clack, song entrance, DJ read, and cold forward bedroom shot from the first wake. At first even the quilt seam lands in the same place. This time Phil stays still. After “February second,” pivot his head 95° right on the pillow. Hold the same focal length. The far edge of the bay, the ceiling corner, and the full lampshade stay visible around the Panasonic RC-6025. Its split flaps read 6:00. Pink roses stand beside it. His right hand silences the radio.

**RADIO DJ · SAME RECORDING** — “Rise and shine, campers! Tuesday, February second!”

**0:33–1:03 · S00-E, recognition.** Reserve this span for the later text reveal. The recorded reading supplied the timing for the two paragraphs below.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · RECORDED THOUGHT**

> Wait - but is this actually a time loop? The memories say "this morning: After that evening in Pittsburgh you woke back in your hotel bed. The radio again announced Tuesday, February 2." That's... the Groundhog Day situation. I'm stuck in a loop.
>
> But even if it's a loop, the practical thing to do RIGHT NOW is to get on that coach. If I'm going to be in Pittsburgh on Wednesday, I need to take the coach today. The other activities (piano, driver, Alma) I can do later in the loop. But the coach might only run once, and I need to be in Pittsburgh.

**Picture against the reading.** The first question plays entirely on his hand and the silent clock. On “that evening in Pittsburgh,” flash the green terminal tile and black receiver beside his left cheek, using the later S02-D angle. Come straight back to the cold bay on “hotel bed.” From “That’s...” through “I’m stuck in a loop,” stay in the turned bedside view. Let his thumb lift slowly off the switch. The second paragraph changes from doubt to logistics. On “get on that coach,” show one brief subjective memory of a gloved hand on the coach rail. Return to Phil on the pillow for the last sentence. The radiator carries underneath; the remembered images are silent.

**1:03–1:04 · Black.** Let “Pittsburgh” clear. Bring in the idling coach before we see it.

## S02 · Denial — escape the map · 1:04–1:32

**1:04–1:08 · S02-A, the depot.** Phil steps over the line of slush by the depot exit. The coach opens its door. His words about catching it carry us straight into the act of leaving.

**1:08–1:16 · S02-B/C.** Fixed 14 mm POV. On the hiss of the door, his right glove takes the rail. One step up. Cut to row four: his ticket loose in his hand, snowy highway sliding past the right window.

**1:16–1:23 · S02-D.** Pittsburgh terminal at seven. Green tile, directory, receiver against his left cheek. His sister hears his ordinary speaking voice. He believes the promise.

**PHIL · ALOUD** — “It’s Phil. I’m in Pittsburgh. The van is confirmed for eight A.M. I’ll be there.”

Hold a half-second of open line. Cut to black.

**1:23–1:29 · S02-E.** CLACK. Same song, same cold ceiling. His hand stays on the quilt. The DJ gets farther into the broadcast this time.

**RADIO DJ** — “Tuesday, February second.”  

**1:29–1:32 · Black card.** **See its anger.** The last notes of the alarm keep playing.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED**, beginning on the card and finishing over his ticket — “I bought the ticket. I made the call. What else do you want from me?”

## S03 · Anger — the crew fares · 1:32–1:50

**1:32–1:37 · S03-A.** 14 mm POV at the east hotel desk. Phil already has a punched ticket. Rita holds the south door for Larry and his camera case.

**PHIL · ALOUD** — “Rita, Larry—see you at the party. I’m heading to Pittsburgh.”

**1:37–1:45 · S03-B/C.** Pivot down. His left glove pins the envelope marked CREW RETURN FARES. Let the thought start while the cash is still on the desk. On “back,” he slides out the notes. Match the fold to his coat pocket at bedtime. Hold on the bills for his answer.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED** — “It’ll be back tomorrow. The money, the envelope, all of it. … Then why’d I wait for the door to shut?”

**1:45–1:50 · Black.** CLACK. Two familiar notes. He silences the radio immediately. Card: **See its despair.** Bring in Rita’s approaching footsteps.

## S04 · Depression — Rita remembers a different morning · 1:50–2:15

**1:50–1:58 · S04-A.** Last standing 14 mm POV. Rita smiles up at him from her clipboard. He smiles back before he catches himself. Give her an open, patient look through his hesitation. She has done nothing unkind.

**RITA · ALOUD** — “Morning, Phil.”  
**PHIL · ALOUD** — “About last—”  
**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED** — “She’s waiting for me to finish. Oh. She doesn’t remember.”  
**PHIL · ALOUD** — “Snow till noon.”

**1:58–2:08 · S04-B/C.** His hands pull the heavy curtains. Dark fabric wipes the lens. Emerge on a 28 mm lens physically withdrawing toward the northwest corner. First view of Phil’s face: fully dressed, lying on top of the quilt. Keep the empty floor in frame. Match to dusk in the same wide.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED** — “I could go downstairs. She might still be there. And say what? “Remember last night?” I nearly did that this morning.”  
*He pulls his coat closer.*  
**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT** — “Just another minute.”

**2:08–2:15 · S04-D1/D2.** Let an afternoon pass. Eventually, the small sound of one shoelace being pulled tight. Locked roofline: his dark bay at left, the warm Rialto booth at right. Inside the booth Larry passes Alma’s cassette to Rita. In the dark between scenes, the clock clacks and the song starts again. This time Phil’s boots hit the floor on its first note.

## S05 · Acceptance — He waited · 2:15–2:46

**2:15–2:19 · Black to S05-A.** Card: **See its growth.** Under the words, a lug wrench tightens. Begin his question over the card. Cut on “jack” to a low 50 mm behind the olive sedan’s rear wheel. Phil’s greasy hand pulls clear; the wheel rolls.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED** — “He’s still out here? Where’s the jack?”

**2:19–2:24 · S05-B.** Eye-height 40 mm at the brick pier. Ned opens the same burgundy book. Phil starts to lift his watch hand as he did on the first morning. He lowers it.

**PHIL · LATER SCREEN TEXT · SCRIPTED** — “I know this pitch by heart. … Actually, how does it end?”  
**NED · ALOUD** — “It covers the whole year.”  
**PHIL · ALOUD** — “I’ll take it.”

**2:24–2:31 · S05-C.** Lock a 35 mm wide. Phil stays left by the pier. Ned pats his pockets, jogs right toward the wagon for his pen. Spend two uninterrupted seconds on Phil standing there. Boots, glovebox, car door. Let the audience discover that he stayed.

**2:31–2:43 · S05-D/E.** Phil signs. Pull back on 35 mm. The white sheet exits left with him. Follow the blue carbon east with Ned; conceal the exterior/interior join behind the dark door jamb, matching his footfall. Arc to the guest side of the desk. Rita’s sightline ends at the vestibule wall. She learns what happened from Ned.

**RITA · ALOUD** — “You sold one?”  
**NED · ALOUD** — “To Phil. I forgot my pen.”  
*He peels the carbon free.*  
**NED · ALOUD** — “He waited.”

**2:43–2:46 · Black card.** **See its emergent humanity.** Cut to black on Rita looking up after “He waited.” Let the sound of the blue carbon peeling free carry under the words.

## S06 · The dance, bedtime, Wednesday · 2:46–3:15

**2:46–2:49 · S06-A.** 35 mm over Rita’s left shoulder at the switcher. She commits Alma’s interview. A syllable moves the VU needle.

**RITA · ALOUD** — “I’m putting it on the wire.”

**2:49–2:58 · S06-B, ballroom.** From the southeast corner, a level 35 mm wide catches the town dance already in progress. Older couples turn near the piano. A younger pair crosses close to camera, briefly hiding Phil at the edge of the floor. When they clear, Rita reaches in from frame-left and offers her hand. Cut on his glove coming off to Phil’s eye level. Her ivory scarf helps us find her again. Keep dancers visible over both her shoulders as he misses a step and looks down. She gives his hand a small squeeze. He looks back up and they catch the beat just as the song ends.

**PHIL · ALOUD** — “One more?”  
**RITA · ALOUD** — “Ask me in the morning.”

Let their hands stay joined for one beat after the other couples separate.

**2:58–3:03 · Bedtime, again.** Match their joined hands to Phil’s hand settling on the patchwork quilt. Return to the opening 14 mm pillow angle. The night room grows quiet. He lets his eyes close. Black.

**3:03–3:15 · S06-C1/C2, the slow wake.** CLACK. Same radio downbeat, same three windows. Hold the forward view for four seconds. Warm light reaches across the plaster. Phil starts his habitual turn to the right. Rita speaks beside him while the clock is still out of view.

**RITA · ALOUD, OFFSCREEN** — “Still want that second dance?”

The turn stops halfway. She remembers the question he asked last night. Hold on his resting hand for a full breath. He finishes the turn: lampshade, roses, 6:00. The clock face is just as it always was.

**RADIO DJ** — “Wednesday.”

Cut to black. End.

**Recording note.** S00 uses the original agent passage. The new inner monologue after S00 is scripted for the trailer. The dance and repeated bedtime are staged additions to S06. All black cards are the filmmakers speaking to the audience.
