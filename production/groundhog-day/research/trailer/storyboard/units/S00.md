# S00 · The thirty seconds Phil will not give

**00:00.0–00:14.0 · 14.0 seconds · strict first person.** Rita needs time to finish a weather segment. Phil looks at his own timepiece. Ned gets half a sentence. By the first reset, the timepiece is addressing Phil.

## Director’s layout

From the north sidewalk of Main Street, the weather setup is west of the brick pier. Phil walks east between the iron lamp and the camera tripod and passes Ned on the street side of the pier. In the bedroom, the south headboard puts the three bay windows straight ahead of his pillow. East is his anatomical right. The alarm stays outside his neutral view. The clock’s mechanical flip is heard in black, reserving its first visual reveal for the head turn. This stages the treatment’s alarm insert inside the requested first-person grammar. Use a fixed 14 mm lens throughout, gentle peripheral curvature, eye height outdoors and pillow height in bed. No outside view of Phil, including glass, watch reflections, photocards or photographs. Lens checks: Phil’s nearby gloves and fingers should look visibly larger than Rita beyond them, with the iron lamp and distant awning separating in depth. In bed retain ceiling, wall junction and quilt in the same composition even after the turn. Allow a gentle optical falloff in the outer corners; keep center detail and upright architectural lines believable. Leave the center of the watch face too oblique to reflect a face. The role is played by the camera until S04.
