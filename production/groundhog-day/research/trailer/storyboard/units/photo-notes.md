# Photo continuity review

Director’s visual checks on the first Seedream photos. Each entry in the [gallery annotations](../photo-qa.json) belongs to a specific image ID. **Ready** means the director considers the photo suitable for the user’s review. The user’s approval is a separate step.

## Opening and policy handoff

The S00-A first take has believable near-to-far depth: a watch is close enough to read against the distant camera and producer. The producer’s face and cue are the trouble. Match the selected Rita identity, give her clear space beside the broadcast camera, and have the gloved right index meet the left watch. Otherwise the audience may take the person in green for another camera operator.

Compare each waking photo to the [original forward plate](../../reference-frames/10_pov_wakeup_no_clock.png). The first S00-D replaced three arched openings with a rectangular window bank. The first S00-E put Phil’s hair, ear and shoulder between the camera and clock. The label **6:00** and small roses can be retained. Frame the new E from the pupils of someone lying on that pillow. A foreshortened right hand and sleeve can enter. His ear and crown cannot.

In the first S05-D2, both men still occupy the center half of the street. Phil’s pale copy and Ned’s burgundy book read, but the frame holds two stationary subjects. For this specific instant, Phil’s coat has almost cleared the left edge. Ned has just put weight on his eastward foot and looks toward the hotel at right. Leave the navy wagon and recessed entrance visible on that side of the pier. The material distinction between the pale original and blue duplicate must survive the pullback.

## The wait and signature

**S05-C1 carries the wrong story.** In the actual first image, Phil in charcoal stands beside the wagon at left and Ned waits underneath the awning at right. A viewer would reasonably conclude Ned did the waiting. Shoot from south of the street, keep Phil at the left-hand brick pier, and send the camel-coated salesman to the right-hand wagon. Once that two-shot is right, use it as the control for the returning-pen endpoint.

The first S05-D1 has the right signing pair. At full resolution a black ballpoint touches the form and the silver watch is on the *same* wrist. A local correction can preserve the photo’s faces, the burgundy book and carbon. Put the watch on the free left wrist and the pen in Phil’s bare right hand.

**S05-E delivers the dialogue image.** Ned matches the street signer. He pulls a blue sheet from the burgundy book beside a clock, and Rita is looking at him. Keep her face as the production identity reference for the opening cue. The film frame sets them on either side of a counter in front of a broad glass entrance; the proposed move lands on the guest side with a recessed, partly shielded entry. For a continuous doorway handoff, carry the hotel master’s door return into this otherwise usable performance.
