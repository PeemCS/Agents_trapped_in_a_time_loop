# S06 · Save those for the morning

**01:34–01:48 · 14 seconds · regional station, ballroom, bedroom.** The last thing Rita heard from Ned was “He waited.” In the next image she has a job to finish. Let the audience see her finish it and then spend a little of her own time on Phil. The morning earns its warmth when she remembers the music before the radio gives the date.

## Places and entrances

The [projection booth](../sets/rialto-booth.svg) is the place where Larry handed Rita the prepared interview in S04. The [station](../sets/regional-station.svg) is another building. By this evening the cassette is at the source deck and Rita is at the north-facing switcher. Its adjacent VU meters are the only on-air cue we need. A cut takes us across town to the [ballroom](../sets/ballroom.svg). Phil is at the same north-wall upright as S01. Rita comes from the west passage and can leave by the same door. End in the [bedroom](../sets/hotel-bedroom.svg). Phil lies against the south headboard with the bay ahead and the clock by his right ear. Keep Rita just outside his framing while she speaks.

## What the audience can infer

Ned remembers who stood waiting for him on this Tuesday. Rita learns it from him in the lobby. The blue carbon can establish the sale. Only Ned can tell her about the wait. Her curiosity earns the visit to Phil. Alma has a claim on Rita’s evening too, established earlier at the booth. Giving both choices screen time keeps the broadcast and the visit hers.

The film proposes that the encounter changes the evening and the loop breaks. The rehearsal evidence for Rita transmitting is take 841, which woke to another Tuesday. Rehearsal 209 reached a new day on a different account of Phil’s actions. The proof reel credits the relay and the two-bar promise as treatment until one run records those steps and its calendar decision. ## Ballroom staging reference

The 1993 festival-banquet photograph catches Phil in profile, bent over the stage keyboard at frame-right. Rita stands at center-left, watching past the edge of the instrument; the teacher and dancers fill out the gathering. A blue stage rim separates Phil from the amber practicals behind Rita. Borrow the clear line between player and listener and the warm depth behind her. For the evening dance, fill the parquet with townspeople of different ages. Two couples turn by the piano and another crosses close to camera while Rita makes her way to Phil. Keep the narrow west approach clear for Rita and her chair. When the dance cuts to Phil’s eyes, hold Rita at arm’s length and keep turning couples visible over both her shoulders. The empty BALL-M photograph establishes the architecture for rehearsal. The evening pickup carries the crowd.
