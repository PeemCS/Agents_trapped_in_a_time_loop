# Building Another Tuesday at the hackathon

Make a 75–110 second film about a TV crew that can leave town. The 1 pm coach reaches Pittsburgh. Phil can call his sister and confirm that he will help move her couch on Wednesday. At six the radio starts up back at the hotel. Establish this geography in three shots. The audience should understand the joke even with the sound off.

Give Rita a story she can finish. Her five minutes on the local station belong to her. She can take Phil’s return, cue Larry’s footage, host herself or leave a station ident on air. Larry has one camera take and a musician he wants to photograph. Phil can have priorities beyond both of them. A possible last shot is Rita moving a fader as the line from Pittsburgh comes alive. Another is a silent line because nobody called. Which one the team can show depends on what the actors actually did.

## What the completed rehearsal changed

Start with the closed clock. Let the crew discover a consequence that can outlive them, such as a family tape actually reaching Pittsburgh. In the original whole-crew rule every tested Phil policy would have got the same delivery reward on the 18 filmed evenings. A stricter letter-return rule gave him real control, but four local small-model casts all passed up the job. Give a new 27B cast a chance to earn that alternative before building a screening around it. ## Cast for behavior

Use Phil, Rita and Larry in the audience-facing version. If using the full identities, put Phil Connors, Rita Hanson and Larry on their crew credentials in Punxsutawney. The qualifying disclosure is simple: these names, jobs and setting are deliberate clues. A spontaneous remark that names the film after he has witnessed the reset is a stronger story than an isolated holiday greeting. The scene still works if he never makes the comparison. The Academy describes the original crew and setting. [Academy screening notes](https://www.oscars.org/events/groundhog-day-25th-anniversary-screening-and-conversation)

A role card gives a character something to do and a reason it matters. Phil’s sister expects him tomorrow. Rita owns a slot and has an independent interest in the band. Larry has to choose what to do with a single charged take. Give every character a way to leave a conversation in the same action as their last line. Speech, intention and arrival are three distinct recorded facts.

A private film quiz measures whether the weights contain the reference. Keep that knowledge available for this version. Editing weights to remove one film would introduce a second training project and remove a source of the joke. Keep prompts, names and the chronology of who first said what so judges can inspect how a specific discovery happened. If Rita supplies an analogy and Phil repeats it, credit Rita.

## The interface that lets the art scale

* Each role gets its own observed facts, legal actions and memory. All three select from the same read-only tick. Resolver commits actions once and delivers local speech and public broadcast results to the right witnesses.
* Persist the event, before/after state and role IDs atomically. Maintain an exact-request journal so a crash after a paid generation can recover that response. Keep the screening projection small enough to go directly into the browser.
* The director sends committed deltas with an index and epoch. A client can reconnect, detect a replaced prefix, and reject a partial or divergent write. The visible sequence can keep playing while inference stalls.
* Draw 30 or 60 visual frames between committed decisions. Deterministic paths, identifiers and camera tracks let a two-dimensional player, an offline editor and a full 3D renderer all reconstruct the same coach journey. Basic walking paths in the film are presentation choreography. In free walk the human supplies the movement.
* The rehearsal exported a real animated OpenUSD stage with town and city sets, three moving character rigs and a native camera. Replace the blocking geometry below those character transforms with detailed assets and separate lighting layers. Preserve actor/event identity and the exact frame of the alarm. [3D stage, measured native render and verification](../research/night/stage3d/README.md)
* An optional trained consideration head can exist beside the language model. Train it on masked pairs with observable costs, requests and consequences. Keep the partner-specific record off the dialogue prompt, and preserve hard physical limits such as a participant withdrawing release for their own tape. Test whether the policy generalizes to a stranger and obeys a refusal before letting it affect a showcase run. [Attachment and evaluation design](attachment-learning.md)

The judgment call for the director is where to cut. Prior work on multi-agent towns supports memory, reflection and interactive places. Prior interactive drama systems show how a director can manage opportunities. ## What changed after the full rehearsal

Open the show with a definite question. Phil thinks the piano might get him out. Can he even meet Rita at the party? Give the audience time to read the first silent note. Show the piano, then cut between the empty depot and Rita dancing. The moment people understand the distance between those rooms, bring in the interview that can actually cross the county line. This version achieved a real 80.25-second, single-world cut with three agent-generated spoken passages. A fresh hero take may yield a different ending. The five checked coach rides are a working montage pattern for an event-day recording. [Screening program](../research/night/screening/index.html#rialto-cut) · [Independent cold projection](../research/night/projection/final-screen.md)

Keep the public-thinking flag on for each actor in the hero world. At four periods per day, a four-day three-role take already contains 48 decisions. The two observed hero casts used 36–37 actual calls each after exact cache reuses and took 15–24 minutes on a contended H100. Use a cached, clearly labeled playable film for the stage show; an inference monitor in a side window can show another draw in progress. A streaming trace with a partial or unclosed final answer gets an unexecuted status. Slot-level journals and a last-good local take make a failed callback recoverable. [Measured service envelope](../research/night/thinking/serving.json)

Choose the film and two alternate takes by the midpoint of the afternoon. Put any experimental 3D character mesh under the logged actor transform. The rehearsal bound an offline GLB wardrobe to a moving actor and imported the same paths into an OpenUSD stage. Render once, then test the exact exported file in a fresh browser. Ruby’s no-store range server supports a cold seek to the last scene. Opening the relative HTML from disk is also a verified path. The sponsor sign-in and a physical HDMI/audio check are the remaining event-side dependencies.
